📝 How to play

🐒 From stranger to friend

  1. Sit face to face and pick Level 1.
  2. Take turns reading each question aloud. Both of you answer it.
  3. Go in order from Level 1 to Level 3. The questions get more personal as you go, so take your time (about 45 minutes in total).
  4. Switch between Tiếng Việt and English anytime, and turn the rain on or off.

🐧 Let me know you more

  1. Pick a topic that matches your mood.
  2. Tap Next for a random question. You won’t see a repeat until every question in that topic has come up.
  3. Change topics whenever the chat needs a new direction.

Ground rules: phones down, be honest, listen more than you talk, and it’s always okay to skip a question. Tip: use ← → keys to move and Esc to close a card.
